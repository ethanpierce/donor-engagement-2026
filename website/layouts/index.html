What we do.

Three programs, one team. Every family gets one person to call, in the language they speak at home.

  • Mentoring

    A mentor for every kid who asks

    Weekly sessions with a trained volunteer mentor. If a kid misses sessions, we call home the same week.

  • Family counseling

    Help when home gets hard

    Private sessions for parents and kids with our counselors. Notes stay private and are written once.

  • School workshops

    Safety and mental health in class

    Workshops for students, fitted to each class and approved by the school, in person or online.
