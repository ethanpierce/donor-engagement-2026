One request, end to end

  1. Maria fills the volunteer check-in on her phone, in Spanish.
  2. The portal posts it in Volunteers and mentions Tidepath Volunteers.
  3. Tidepath Volunteers logs 1 hour in the shared CRM, once, and flags “needs a laptop” as urgent.
  4. Tidepath Director routes the need to Programs and mentoring and mentions Alex.
  5. Tidepath Programs sees Kevin on this week’s at-risk list and drafts a Spanish note to his mother.
  6. Alex approves in the People CRM app. Nothing goes out without that approval.

Known gaps

Replies stay in Roomcord

Agents draft messages to parents and schools. Sending them by SMS or email is not built.

CRM app shows demo data

The People CRM tab is built from the seed data. It does not read the live shared CRM yet.

Generic RoomAgent noise

The platform re-adds a generic agent to every room. Silencing it needs a code change.

Not a HIPAA system

Counseling uses made-up notes only. Real notes must stay in an approved system.

Portal hardening

Approval links are not signed and the rate limit resets on restart. Haitian Creole text needs a native review.
