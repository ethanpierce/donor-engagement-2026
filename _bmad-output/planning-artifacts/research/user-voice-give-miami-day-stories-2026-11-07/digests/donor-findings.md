# Donor findings — Give Miami Day site + its platform docs

Accessed 2026-11-07. Each line: claim — source. "GMD" = Give Miami Day.

## A. Donors who stop giving
- A1. "More than half of your Donors will not give to your organization next year. Only 2/10 first time Donors will give again next year." Reasons named: passed away, can't afford it, or "poor service or communication." — GMD guide *Loving Your Donors & Champions* (Resources Library, https://bit.ly/4y6DEQX)
- A2. Returning nonprofits can "identify retained and lapsed donors" with the platform's reporting tools. — GMD Nonprofit FAQ, https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/orgFAQ
- A3. The Retention report filters donors as "Retained" or "Not Retained", can be exported, and lets the nonprofit email a donor from the row. It is only on the paid Essentials or Accelerate plans. — Mightycause help, *Donation Management and Reports*, https://support.mightycause.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017259552
- A4. Donors get a "Give Again — Refill My Cart" button to repeat last year's gifts. — GMD *How do I give?*, https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/give
- A5. Context, web: overall US donor retention 43.3%, one-time donors 31.9% (Fundraising Effectiveness Project Q4 2025, via secondary summary — not verified at source). No GMD-specific retention figure found. — web search 2026-11-07, e.g. https://bloomerang.com/blog/donor-retention

## B. Thank-yous and follow-up
- B1. "Thank early and often… Call, email or text people as they give." Have the team member closest to the donor reach out. — *Loving Your Donors* guide
- B2. Thank again the day after GMD; "No gift is too small; personalize." — same
- B3. New donors: a 3-email welcome series, every other week or more, first one a thank-you; "If possible, use a CRM system to automate." — same
- B4. Quarterly impact reports beat annual ones. — same
- B5. "Invite to Give Again… People will give to your cause more than once a year. So ask them." — same
- B6. Personal video thank-yous (phone or BombBomb/ThankView), sent by the person closest to the donor. — same
- B7. Platform has an "Automated Emails" tool to contact donors based on how they used the GMD page, and a "Contacts" feature with donation history. — GMD Resources Library, https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/resources-library
- B8. Nonprofits should use the Donation Report "to thank your donors." — GMD Nonprofit FAQ

Note: the guide gives no evidence that calls raise retention. It is advice, not a study. Our earlier RCT finding (plain thank-you calls did not raise retention) still stands.

## C. Monthly / recurring gifts
- C1. Donors can pick recurring giving on GMD; later gifts are paid out "as they are processed according to the donor's selected giving schedule." Recurring donors carry a "recurring gift designation" in the donation report. — GMD Nonprofit FAQ
- C2. Monthly gifts charge the same day each month. The donor gets an email before each charge and a receipt after. The receipt does not say "recurring." — Mightycause help, https://support.mightycause.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015176772
- C3. The nonprofit's Recurring Donations report shows status **Active** or **Canceled** only, plus counts of active and canceled. — Mightycause help, https://support.mightycause.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024641091

## D. Failed or expiring cards
- D1. A "!" next to an Active recurring gift means "the credit card on file is about to expire." Mightycause emails the donor, but "we recommend directly reaching out to the donor." — Mightycause help, articles 360024641091 and 360017259552
- D2. So the nonprofit learns about an expiring card only if someone opens the report and spots the "!". No alert to the nonprofit is described. — inference from D1 + C3 (gap, not a claim)
- D3. No documented "failed" status, retry schedule, or nonprofit alert for a declined monthly charge was found on GMD or Mightycause help. — absence finding, searched 2026-11-07
- D4. Donor FAQ: if a gift won't go through, call your bank; repeated tries can trip fraud protection and lock the card. — GMD Donor FAQ, https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/DonorFaq
- D5. Other platforms (web, not GMD): retry 3/5/7 days after the charge date; some email the nonprofit only after the last failed try; expired card is the most common cause. — e.g. https://support.raisely.com/article/596-understanding-regular-giving-failures-and-retries, https://help.commitchange.com/article/90-when-a-recurring-donation-fails

## E. How small teams track donors / data pain
- E1. Nonprofits can add offline gifts (cash, check) by hand with name, email, source, notes. — Mightycause help 360017259552
- E2. Reports are "time-sensitive"; confirm the date range. GMD totals in the dashboard differ from the payout report (fees, bonuses). — GMD Nonprofit FAQ
- E3. 5K runners who choose a nonprofit land on a separate team roster; nonprofits get it from a Team Captain or by email request. — GMD 5K page, https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/5KFAQ  → one more intake list, like Denise's four.
- E4. Stories show teams of 1–2 staff or a founder working from home with volunteers (Nora Sandigo, Exchange for Change, Hued Songs). — story pages
