# What Give Miami Day tells us about Problem 2 (donors who quietly stop giving)

Type: user-voice · Run mode · accessed 2026-11-07 · source: https://www.givemiamiday.org/p/stories and every linked section, plus the platform's help docs.
Details and every citation: `digests/donor-findings.md`. List of all nonprofits and pages: `digests/index-nonprofits-and-pages.md`.

## The short version

1. **The stories are silent on donor follow-up.** All 9 nonprofit stories talk about what the money bought. None says how they track, thank, or win back donors. The groups are tiny: 1–2 staff, or a founder working from home. That matches Denise. The silence suggests nobody has time for this job.

2. **Losing donors is normal and big.** Give Miami Day's own guide says more than half of donors won't give next year, and only 2 in 10 first-time donors come back. One named reason is "poor service or communication" — the part we can fix. (*Loving Your Donors* guide)

3. **The tool already sorts "retained" vs "not retained" — but only once a year, and only if you pay.** The platform's Retention report needs a paid plan. It shows who didn't come back after the fact. It does not warn before a donor is late. That is our gap: **a heads-up while there's still time to call.** (Mightycause help; GMD Nonprofit FAQ)

4. **Expiring cards show as a tiny "!" in a report.** Mightycause emails the donor. The nonprofit sees nothing unless someone opens the report. A monthly gift has only two statuses: Active or Canceled. We found no "failed" status and no alert to the nonprofit for a declined charge. (Mightycause help)

5. **The advice is "thank fast, thank personally, ask again."** Call, email, or text on the day. Thank again the next day. Send new donors 3 welcome emails. Use a personal video. Invite them to give more than once a year. This is advice, not proof — it has no study behind it. Our earlier study (plain thank-you calls didn't help) still stands. (*Loving Your Donors* guide)

6. **Giving days create a yearly rhythm.** Give Miami Day is Nov 19, 2026, with a "Give Again" button for last year's donors. A donor who gives every giving day, or every December, is an "annual regular." The due date is easy to guess. (GMD *How do I give?*; Nonprofit FAQ)

## What it means for our plan

- **Keep:** guess each donor's due date and flag them before it passes. The platform only reports after the fact.
- **Add:** watch for cards about to expire, not only failed ones. The "!" exists, but nobody looks at it.
- **Add:** for annual donors, tie the due date to the event they gave at (Give Miami Day, December, the gala).
- **Careful:** a thank-you call alone may not keep donors. The note should give news ("here's what your gift did") or ask again, not just say thanks.
