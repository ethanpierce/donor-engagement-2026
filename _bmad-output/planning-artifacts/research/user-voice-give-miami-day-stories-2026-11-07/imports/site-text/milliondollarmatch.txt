 What's behind our Million Dollar Match? | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/7872965e-1a2d-459c-8c5a-45c76bfc8a26/ | We sat down with the anonymous sponsor of our Million Dollar Match to find out why they give back to our community through Give Miami Day, and why they choose to do so through a Million Dollar Match. Since 2022, all organizations participating in Give Miami Day are awarded up to an additional $1,000 through the match. | Q: What initially inspired you to fund the Million-Dollar Match, and what has kept you coming back? A: We’re thrilled to be sponsors of the million-dollar match! Our inspiration started with a spur-of-the-moment gift to the Give Miami Day Bonus Pool back in 2021, during the very final hour of the day. We had dollars that we wanted to give but we weren’t sure where. We thought it would be both easy and fun to amplify the gifts of all Give Miami Day donors—plus it was nearly midnight and we were running out of time! So, we logged in and entered a gift of $100,000 to the General Bonus Pool, to be divided among all charities in proportion to their received donations. It was the largest single donation we’d ever made and a large chunk of the total Bonus Pool funding that year, but we didn’t really think too much of it, or expect to hear anything back, or for it to drive our future gifting. Soon after the event, we received a very thoughtful and kind email from Rebecca, the head of the Miami Foundation, that helped us to see how special and unique the gift was. Overall, we felt a greater connection and sense of purpose from this gift than any other that we’d made previously. We felt connected to the entire Miami community—connected to tens of thousands of donors whose donations we’d helped amplify, to the thousand local organizations that would benefit, to the event host in the Miami Foundation, and to the annual event and community movement of Give Miami Day. It felt great and we knew we’d be back. | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/6d0d576c-6c57-4511-8689-f3d4c3120354/-/crop/2738x3650/60,0/-/preview/ | scale-100 | 136-L1030221.jpg | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/642fa370-2ed9-4acf-b619-84ad34121a80/ | Q: Explain to us how the match works and why have you chosen to structure your giving in this way. A: The match started with a simple idea: matching the first $1,000 of donations that each organization receives on Give Miami Day. We proposed this to the Miami Foundation for Give Miami Day in 2022, and then we all did the math together. With over a thousand organizations participating, we realized this could become a million-dollar match—whoa! It was a big commitment, but we were excited to make it happen. Working with the Miami Foundation team, we refined how it works. We match the first $25 of each donation, which helps spread the impact across more donors. Since $25 is the minimum donation on Give Miami Day, this means we’re always matching $25 per donor, and organizations need at least 40 donors to unlock their full $1,000 match. This structure especially encourages smaller organizations to grow their donor base. In previous years, after covering this base match for Give Miami Day, we’ve had significant dollars remaining from the million-dollar commitment. We’ve chosen to use these extra funds to increase the match for Small organizations beyond $25 per donor, since the match often makes the biggest difference for these organizations as a larger percentage of their total fundraising. | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/3d9b2447-938a-4faa-b17d-40de3a00780c/ | scale-100 | tempImage2t0biO.heic | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/c650b4aa-33a1-4867-941c-c2f418536f66/-/crop/1364x1366/343,0/-/preview/ | scale-100 | 1.png | Q: Generosity and giving back to your community are clearly important in your life. Can you share a little bit about your wider philanthropic journey and philosophy? A: From volunteering with various organizations in high school and college to giving small donations as we were starting our careers, philanthropy in one form or another has always been a part of our lives. We are entrepreneurs in the tech space and have been very fortunate with the success of our company. As our assets grew, we felt a strong desire to do more with our philanthropy but weren’t sure exactly what or how. This feeling increased as we started to raise a family and hoped to both set a good example for our young children and give towards a better future for them. We made various donations as we came across opportunities, but didn’t always feel a strong connection to the individual causes or a greater movement. We’re a few years in now with the Give Miami Day match and while we’re still finding our path on our philanthropic journey, the Miami Foundation and Give Miami Day have helped to give us a strong footing. We love Give Miami Day and the idea that we can inspire other donors and help lift so many amazing nonprofits all at once. | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/ba72a296-be3a-4a10-a883-2dcd04fb4ee9/-/crop/4029x4032/1005,0/-/preview/ | scale-100 | Q: Why fund this match anonymously? Why is anonymity important to you? A: Giving anonymously has been a very personal decision for us. Mostly it’s been a question of privacy, and a concern that a gift of this size could bring some unneeded or unwanted attention. Only a handful of people know the extent of our assets or charitable giving, and for now, we prefer to keep it that way. That said, anonymity feels especially appropriate for this particular gift. We think of it as a Community Match, and an extension of the Give Miami Day General Bonus Pool, rather than our personal or family match. We know it might seem ironic to say this in the context of sharing our story, but we genuinely prefer the spotlight to be on the broader community—especially on this special day that brings everyone together. | https://imagecdn.mightycause.com/1c2f81c5-816d-4a30-b443-dd9faaf3a0c4/-/crop/1364x1366/202,0/-/preview/ | scale-100 | tempImageMYFXmT.heic | Q: Overall, what do you wish more people knew or understood about Give Miami Day?
