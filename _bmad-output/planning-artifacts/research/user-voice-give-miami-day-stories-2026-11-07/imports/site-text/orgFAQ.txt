Be one of the following eligible organization types: • Tax-exempt 501(c)(3) public charity • Private operating foundation • Educational institution • Religious organization • Government social benefit agency (public works, parks, animal services, etc.) • Project with an established IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor 2. Offer programming in Miami-Dade County. Pre-approved philanthropic funds housed at The Miami Foundation that support programming in Miami-Dade County are also eligible to participate. 3. Be actively registered with the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services. While we do not collect your organization's Certificate of Solicitation, maintaining an active registration is required by the State of Florida. Organizations are responsible for ensuring they remain in compliance. You can learn more about the Solicitation of Contributions Act and related requirements through the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services . Any questions regarding the Certificate of Solicitation should be directed to that agency. The following organizations are not eligible to participate in Give Miami Day (though we deeply appreciate the important work they do): • Private non-operating foundations (IRS Form 990-PF filers) • Type III supporting organizations (IRS charity status 509(a)(3)) • Advocacy organizations with IRS charity status 501(c)(4) • Chambers of commerce and membership organizations with IRS charity status 501(c)(6) • Public safety organizations with IRS charity status 509(a)(4) | Who is eligible to participate? | In Florida, by law, all charitable organizations must be registered with the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services to legally solicit donations from donors within the state of Florida. These funds increase the impact of donations made during the campaign, giving participating nonprofits the opportunity to receive more than the dollars donated directly to them. The Miami Foundation fundraises for two types of Bonus Pools: General Bonus Pool: All participating organizations qualify for the general bonus pool percentage match. The bonus gift will be calculated by dividing the total general bonus pool dollars by the total eligible gifts (between $25 and $10,000; gifts above $10,000 are welcome; the first $10,000 counts toward a Bonus Pool boost ) made to organizations. The Miami Foundation determines the Bonus Pool percentage at the conclusion of Give Miami Day because, like you, we are fundraising all the way through Give Miami Day. Primary Category Bonus Pool: During the registration process, organizations select the primary category that aligns most with their work.
