After Give Miami Day ends, The Miami Foundation works out a match percentage. The total money raised for the Bonus Pool is divided by the total eligible donations across all organizations. That percentage is then applied to boost each organization’s donations. Only donations between $25 and $10,000 count toward that calculation. People can still give more than $10,000, but only the first $10,000 of a gift counts toward the Bonus Pool boost. Organizations also qualify for an Issue Area Bonus Pool based on the primary issue area selected during registration. The ten issue areas are: Animals, Arts & Culture, Community Care, Economic & Community Development, Education & Youth, Environment, Faith Based, Health & Wellness, Human Rights, Advocacy, & Civic Engagement, and Inclusion & Accessibility. The bonus percentage for each issue area is determined by dividing the total dollars raised for that Issue Area Bonus Pool by the total amount of eligible donations made to organizations within that category. Because The Miami Foundation continues fundraising for Bonus Pools through the end of Give Miami Day, final Bonus Pool percentages cannot be calculated until all giving has concluded. Once final totals are confirmed, Bonus Pool funds are distributed to participating organizations based on the eligible donations they received. | How do Bonus Pools work? | Prizes are additional opportunities for participating nonprofits to win extra funding during the Give Miami Day campaign.
