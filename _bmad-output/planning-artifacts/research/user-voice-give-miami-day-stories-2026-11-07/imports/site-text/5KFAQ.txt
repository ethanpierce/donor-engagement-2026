Runners have the opportunity to donate to the issue area they care about the most, or route their registration fee directly to a particular nonprofit (you). If they choose your nonprofit, they become a part of your race group, and you can then see who is supporting you. Nonprofits that are not registered to fundraise through Give Miami Day 2026 are ineligible to participate or receive donations from The Give Miami 5K. Once a nonprofit has completed their Give Miami Day registration, it will take approximately 24 – 48 hours to set up a running team on The Give Miami Day 5K race registration page. Nonprofits will receive the donations raised from The Give Miami 5K as part of their Give Miami Day payout. The amount raised will be reflected on their Give Miami Day profile on Give Miami Day (November 19, 2026). | Nonprofit 5K Team Captains | What is a Give Miami 5K Team Captain? A Team Captain is the primary point of contact for your nonprofit’s Give Miami 5K team. Team Captains help recruit runners, encourage participation, share race updates with their team, and build excitement leading up to race day. Do Team Captains need to register for the Give Miami 5K? Yes. Being designated as your nonprofit's Team Captain does not automatically register you for the race. If you plan to participate, you'll need to complete the Give Miami 5K registration like any other runner. During registration, be sure to select your nonprofit so you're added to your organization's team How do I become a Team Captain? When completing your nonprofit's Give Miami Day registration, you'll be asked to identify a Team Captain. The Team Captain can be changed at any time by contacting 5kfestival@miamifoundation.org. What are the responsibilities of a Team Captain? Team Captains serve as the primary point of contact for their nonprofit's Give Miami 5K team. They can share their organization's unique team registration link with supporters, encourage participation, and build excitement leading up to race day. Team Captains can also view who has registered to support their nonprofit, making it easy to track participation and engage their team throughout the campaign. Can a nonprofit have more than one Team Captain? Each nonprofit may designate one Team Captain. Your Team Captain will have access to your organization's unique team registration link to share with supporters and can view who has registered to support your nonprofit. If you need to change your Team Captain after registration, please contact 5kfestival@miamifoundation.org for assistance. Will Team Captain’s be able to see who has joined their team? Yes.
