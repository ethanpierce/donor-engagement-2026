WELCOME!
Give Miami Day is more than a day of giving; it's an opportunity to tell your story, deepen
relationships with our community, and inspire generosity.

Whether you're joining for the first time or building on past campaigns, this guide is designed to
help you navigate the journey with confidence.

We've curated the essential resources, tools, and tutorials into one easy-to-use guide so you
can spend less time searching and more time focusing on what matters most: advancing your
mission.

Explore the sections below to find everything you need to register your organization, take
advantage of Capacity Building opportunities, and build your campaign through the Knowledge
Center.

This guide isn't meant to be read from start to finish. Think of it as your bookmark throughout
Give Miami Day to remind you about your next steps, discover a new resource, or deepen your
knowledge toward a stronger campaign.

Inside you'll find key information about:

   Registration
   Capacity Building Opportunities
   Knowledge Center

Since 2012, Give Miami Day has raised over $230 million for more than 1,000 nonprofits across
Greater Miami, growing from $7.2 million raised in 2015 to $43.8 million in 2025. The number of
donors giving on this day has grown by 251% in just the last decade.

Give Miami Day is proof of what this city can do when it comes together.

We're cheering you on every step of the way.

CAPACITY AND
COMMUNITY BUILDING
The Give Miami Day Capacity & Community Building Program is designed to equip
nonprofit leaders with the knowledge, tools, and connections they need to build
stronger campaigns and stronger organizations.

Throughout the Give Miami Day Season, you'll have access to workshops, coaching
sessions, peer learning opportunities, and practical resources that support every
stage of your Give Miami Day journey.

Whether you're participating for the first time or looking to take your campaign to
the next level, this program is here to help you grow your fundraising capacity,
strengthen your strategy, and connect with a community of nonprofit leaders
working toward a more generous Miami.

We believe that when nonprofits learn together, share ideas, and invest in one
another, the entire community is stronger.

How can organizations participate?

   Attending Workshops to strengthen practical fundraising, communications,
   and campaign planning skills.
   Joining Strategy Sessions for group coaching, peer learning, and collaborative
   problem-solving.
   Connecting with Fellow Nonprofit Leaders through community-building
   experiences that foster collaboration and shared learning.
   Applying What You Learn by putting new strategies into practice and
   strengthening your organization's fundraising efforts.

KNOWLEDGE CENTER
The Give Miami Day Knowledge Center is your go-to resource for learning, planning,
and growing throughout your Give Miami Day journey. Designed to support nonprofit
leaders at every stage of the campaign, the Knowledge Center brings together curated
resources, practical tools, on-demand learning, and expert guidance, all in one place.

The Knowledge Center allows you to explore content at your own pace, revisit
materials whenever you need them, and discover new strategies that can strengthen
your Give Miami Day campaign.

What You'll Find

Give Miami Day Informational Resources: Everything you need to understand
Give Miami Day and navigate the campaign with confidence, from registration
guides and campaign checklists to platform tutorials and key campaign updates.

Fundraising & Strategic Resources: Build a stronger fundraising strategy with
practical guides, templates, worksheets, and videos covering campaign planning,
budgeting, matching gifts, board engagement, peer-to-peer fundraising, and more.

Marketing and Storytelling Resources: Strengthen your storytelling and
communications with branding toolkits, messaging resources, campaign templates,
and best practices to help you engage supporters and amplify your impact.

Donor Cultivation & Relationship Building: Develop meaningful donor
relationships before, during, and after Give Miami Day through resources focused on
donor acquisition, stewardship, engagement, and long-term relationship building.
