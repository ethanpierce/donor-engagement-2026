                                LOVING YOUR DONORS AND CHAMPIONS

                                 Best Practices for Showing Love
                                  Motivation to Spend More Time Loving Your Donors:
                                  Part 1: People feel good when they give. They have the motivation to be part of something
                                  bigger than themselves. Everyone is a Donor!
                                  Part 2: Gratitude has scientifically been shown to be good for one's health! What better thing to
                                  give to your Donors than gratitude.
                                  Part 3: Data shows that more than half of your Donors will not give to your organization next
                                  year. Only 2/10 first time Donors will give again next year. People stop giving because they
                                  either passed away, they can no longer afford it, or they received poor service or
                                  communication.
                                  While we can't change someone's ability to give, we can control how we work with those
                                  people over time. You can continue to love them and keep them involved.


                                 Top 12 Tips
                                1 Change Your Thinking
                                     Appropriately balance Donor stewardship and Donor relationship activities. Stewardship
                                     activities are done in service to fulfill the promises made to the Donor. Relationship
                                     activities are things to nurture the connection.


                               2 Thank Early and Often
                                     Monitor donations closely. Call, email or text people as they give during Early Giving and on
                                     the day of Give Miami Day.
                                     Take this opportunity to strengthen your relationship with your Donor. Have a team
                                     member with a close connection to the Donor reach out and give them a heartfelt thank
                                     you that touches on the immediate impact of their gift.

                               3 Thank Again on Friday
                                     Send additional appreciation notes to Donors and Supporters after Give Miami Day. No gift
                                     is too small; personalize your communications.
                                     Communicate with your Donors as though they were your friends and family not just
                                     transactions.

                               4 Provide Updates Quickly
                                     Tell Donors what their gifts will accomplish the day of their donation and the day after. At
                                     the end of the year thank them again.
                                     Share updates with Champions; what their efforts raised and how it will impact your
                                     mission.

                               5 Use Video
                                     Create and send a video that can pertain to every Donor. Separate videos can be created
                                     for different Donor categories, like those that donate a higher amount.
                                     Videos can be phone recorded, or you can use services like BombBomb and ThankView.
                                     Record pre GMD videos that include a soft ask talking about the impact of a Donor's gift.
                                     After GMD, have a team member with the closest relationship to the Donor send a
                                     personalized video specifically about their donation.



                               6 Grab Bag to Thank Champions
                                     Name Champions (with pictures) in your newsletter.
                                     Send a dedicated email publicly thanking Champions.
                                     Send and or post Thank Yous on social media.
                                     Send Branded items / swag boxes to Champions.
                                     Throw events and gatherings to invite Champions during GMD week, like a Happy Hour or
                                     brunch.

                               7 Welcome New Donors
                                     Welcome and thank new Donors brought in by Give Miami Day. Create a first time Donor
                                     welcome email series with the goal of bringing the Donor closer and informing them about
                                     your organization.
                                     Send 3 emails in total every other week or more, with the first being a Thank You and all of
                                     them including more information about your organization.
                                     If possible, use a CRM system to automate sending out these emails.
                                     This is a great practice to do year round not just with Give Miami Day.

                               8 Quarterly Impact Reports
                                     Opt for creating Quarterly Impact Reports over annual reports as they are more effective.
                                     Focus on how gifts made a difference to your mission and constituents. Share data and
                                     accompanying stories.

                               9 Donors Only Events
                                     Create Donors-only experiences. Do an activity that is appropriate to your mission and
                                     budget to bring Donors together and thank them.
                                     Create separate events based on Donor categories, like for higher dollar Donors.
                                     These events become cultivation opportunities. Think smaller more intimate gatherings to
                                     nurture relationships.


                               10 Engage Your Champions
                                     After Give Miami Day, ask your Champions questions about their experience. What worked
                                     well? What could be improved? How can we make the experience easier next year? They
                                     will appreciate being asked and you can implement their answers into next year's plan.


                               11 Invite to Give Again
                                     Engage with your Supporters more than just on Give Miami Day. People will give to your
                                     cause more than once a year. So ask them.

                               12 Adopt a Mindset
                                     Surprise and delight your Donors! When are those moments you can surprise people, make
                                     them feel seen, and do something out of the ordinary?
